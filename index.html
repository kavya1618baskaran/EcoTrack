About EcoTrack

EcoTrack is a smart environmental sustainability tracking platform developed to encourage individuals to adopt eco-friendly habits and make responsible environmental decisions. In today's world, environmental problems such as climate change, water scarcity, air pollution, excessive energy consumption and increasing waste have become major challenges. Many of these problems are connected to our everyday activities. EcoTrack helps users understand how their daily choices affect the environment and motivates them to make positive changes. Through simple tracking tools, personalized progress monitoring and environmental challenges, EcoTrack aims to make sustainable living easier, smarter and more meaningful.

🌍
